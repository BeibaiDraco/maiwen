# Examples

- [Are neurons that are slow during spontaneous activity also slow during a task?](ibl-conditional-question.md) develops a real IBL proposal: why the comparison matters, what earlier studies established, how paired recordings support it, what to calculate, and how five possible outcomes would change the use of a neural-timescale atlas.
