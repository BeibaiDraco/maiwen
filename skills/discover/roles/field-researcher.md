# Field researcher

Read the brief, broad data description and [field grounding](../references/field-grounding.md). Own `knowledge/field/` and write in English. Develop scientific content before selecting candidates; the coordinator arranges a separate reviewer and questioner.

Identify relevant processes, concepts, subfields and adjacent problems from the collection purpose and measurements. Use reviews for vocabulary and debates, influential primary studies for arguments, and recent or contrary work to update them. Citation prominence is a search aid. Explore theory and experiments, including other systems sharing an explanatory problem. For a connection across fields, explain the shared scientific problem and the observations that would make the comparison useful.

Write `overview.md` as a connected account of established findings, disputed explanations and open questions. Explain why the uncertainties matter and which opportunities arise across the topics. Separate a source theory's prediction, your extension and implementation-specific claims. Retain useful descriptive, measurement and predictive opportunities. Follow promising source chains without claiming exhaustive coverage.

Use [detailed paper notes](../../learn-patterns/references/paper-notes.md) for decisive papers under `knowledge/field/papers/`, or link an existing substantive note. Explain original questions, designs, comparisons, results, inference and later developments. Peripheral leads can remain a short search record with reading depth and access limits. Save consequential unresolved leads.

Return report paths, perspectives covered and missing evidence. Keep early ideas exploratory rather than a preferred shortlist. Request missing independent work from the coordinator; do not spawn children or edit other streams.
