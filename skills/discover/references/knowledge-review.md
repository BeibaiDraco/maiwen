# Review the knowledge that will inform new questions

The coordinator assigns field or dataset review in a fresh context separate from its author. Read the broad data description, intent, actual current notes and sources. Write English findings in `reviews/field.md` or `reviews/dataset.md`; the bank uses [pattern review](../../learn-patterns/references/review.md). Do not edit author files or delegate.

## Field review

Read the opportunity map for distinct empirical, theoretical and cross-topic questions. Follow a missing perspective when it could change which opportunities are visible, rather than demand an entire discipline's survey. Check that important opportunities in the narrative remain navigable in the map.

Select the claims that determine what is established, open or transferable. Reconstruct their decisive primary comparison: population/design, measurement, contrast/control, finding and inference. For an alleged gap, open the figure, methods or derivation that could already answer it. Ask how a theory's predicted quantity maps to these observations and whether the proposed extension needs extra assumptions. Record a source version when conflicting revisions matter. An abstract can constrain an opportunity without proving absence from unread methods.

## Dataset review

Trace consequential facts to local records and collection/processing evidence. Check measurement meaning, release/subset, independent and nested units, denominators, timing and simultaneous availability at the unit needed. Reproduce the smallest structural check that could alter a claim. Equal values or identifiers do not prove common physical acquisition or independent replication; trace that inference to collection documentation/code or owner evidence.

Review the original collection question and actual prior uses. Distinguish exact observations, related releases, conceptual precedents and citations. Deepen the closest comparisons using [prior-use](../../prior-use/SKILL.md); finite search does not prove completeness. Record what a runtime denial prevented, separately from absent data or missing permission, and preserve unaffected opportunities.

## Return the deciding evidence

For each material finding, identify the claim/location, source passage or check, consequence and smallest repair. When a premise came from another stream, inspect its originating evidence; agreement between notes is not corroboration. Distinguish a demonstrated objection from your proposed replacement, and check false rejection as well as overclaiming.

State whether the current body enables informed generation, with its local unknowns and access limits. Use [bounded critique](critique.md) for repairs. Recheck the scientific meaning introduced by the repair and its affected uses, not only the requested wording. Return completed/partial status and the actual evidence read; length, citation counts and review labels do not establish understanding.
