# Use independent review to improve the scientific argument

Use the existing independent responsibilities in [coordination](coordination.md), within the shared budget. Give initial reviewers the actual scientific documents, sources, permitted checks, exposure and scope, without earlier verdicts or preferred answers. Disclose unavoidable verdicts embedded in sources. Write findings in English. Support each judgment with source evidence, a calculation or an inspectable argument.

## Check the argument at its point of use

Knowledge reviewers assess whether the prepared accounts support informed question generation, with their remaining uncertainties identified. Screeners compare candidate contributions with decisive primary passages. Planners begin with the uncertainty that could change investment. Plan reviewers then follow the strongest conclusion through its complete computation using [answerability](answerability.md). Reuse these responsibilities instead of adding another review tier.

An initial assignment can say:

> Assess [current artifact] against [sources, permitted inputs and scope] within [budget]. Identify the strongest scientific claim and the weakest unverified step that supports it. Perform the smallest adequate check of that step. Explain the consequence, what remains worth learning, and any other decision that prevents a faithful handoff. Check unjustified rejection as well as acceptance.

## Repair, verify and stop

Record the allowance in `brief.md` or the standalone bank index. Default to an initial assessment plus at most **two substantive author-repair/verification cycles per knowledge stream or linked question argument**, within the total budget and with delivery time reserved. Renaming, reframing, changing reviewers or entering final writing does not reset consumed cycles. Shared-premise repairs follow the affected arguments. Editorial corrections need no scientific cycle.

Use the existing review note to explain the objection, deciding evidence, author response, changed scientific meaning and verification. Keep the explanation in that note so the reader can follow the correction.

1. **Establish the objection and choose the repair.** The author checks the cited source, measurement or inference. Distinguish evidence for the objection from a reviewer's proposed remedy. Retain supported disagreement. When adopting a change, state what new proposition it asserts and what worthwhile question remains; a factual correction can introduce a stronger unsupported explanation.
2. **Update the current argument.** Revise the affected calculation, claim and current source note. Tell the coordinator which questions and delivery passages depend on it. Preserve the previous account as history with a clear successor. After a substantial reframe, recheck the contribution, literature and required data. Justify the revised question scientifically, including when it uses more observations or a different decision rule.
3. **Test the replacement independently.** The reviewer receives the changed argument, original finding and check evidence. Start with the resulting scientific implication. Test its actual rule against the original failure and a relevant different failure when needed, with an intended valid case where useful. Inspect newly strengthened claims and affected downstream uses. Report what now follows and what remains unverified; identify the actual evidence for closing each finding, leaving promised or unfinished checks pending.

Collect completed or explicit partial returns through native result collection, then read the evidence. File appearance does not establish completion. A recheck can reuse the reviewer and is not a blind initial assessment.

Continue only while the next affordable check could change a worthwhile scientific decision. An unspent cycle is not an obligation to use it. Stop at the allowance or total budget, or earlier for unavailable evidence, insufficient remaining value or no decision-relevant progress. Record the actual reason; exhaustion alone establishes neither rejection nor acceptance. At the limit, correct overclaims and deliver supported partials without introducing a new unverified deciding rule. Carry consumed cycles and the smallest unresolved decision into NEXT. A genuinely different queued opportunity may receive remaining capacity, but the same argument cannot restart under a new name.

## Check the finished document

Give the existing final independent reader the current `recommendations.md`, all final plans, `NEXT.md`, prepared English summary and linked evidence. Earlier reviews do not cover newly written scientific choices. Begin by comparing what a new colleague would infer from the opening and payoff with what the actual computation can establish.

Follow each main claim through its measurement, population, weights, estimator and interpretation. Check whether the report, summary or NEXT adds a mechanism, absence, timing, independence or readiness claim that the plan does not support. Revise any affected sentence to express the inference the computation actually supports. Use a decisive source, logical or executable check when necessary; identify exactly what cannot be verified within the remaining scope.

Use [implementation handoff](implementation-handoff.md) to expose scientific choices an implementer would have to invent. Check current notes loaded by NEXT for superseded premises. Preserve positive explanations of the exact question, importance and computation; use [delivery](delivery.md) for readable writing rather than repeat precautionary lists. Distinguish scientific support from computational completeness.

Record reviewed paths/revisions and findings in `final-review.md`. Apply the same repair allowance to substantive changes, including final-reviewer suggestions. Compare the final deliverables with the checked account before sending; a substantive rewrite needs recheck or explicit pending status. Save the checked English summary, and preserve its scientific meaning in any conversational translation. Keep the final message to the outcome, links to the report and the next decision. Later corrections remain later corrections; do not backfill acceptance.
