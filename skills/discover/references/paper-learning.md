# Apply reusable question patterns to this project

Use [learn-patterns](../../learn-patterns/SKILL.md) to build, review or reuse the bank before formal generation. Source cases and cross-paper patterns belong in the portable library; this project records their applicability in `knowledge/pattern-selection.md`. Read the actual pattern and relevant case, including its review scope and essential conditions. A citation is not a reasoning transfer.

Before a candidate exists, connect useful question-forming and hypothesis-forming moves to broad data affordances and unresolved conditions without prescribing an answer. State which kind of help the existing bank actually supplies. When reusing an older bank, supplement a missing relevant move from its cases or new sources under review rather than relabeling the entire bank as freshly rebuilt. For a candidate later credited to paper learning, explain: source feature X served inferential role R; target feature Y may or may not serve R; condition Z remains necessary; therefore ask or revise Q. Link the source's decisive result or methods and verify target correspondence in the data. Separate source conclusion, your interpretation and proposed extension.

Name the actual branch/operation selected from a broad pattern and why it fits the scientific starting point. Try a concrete failure case for any transfer that determines the question, distinguishing a missing essential condition from an alternative explanation that survives when the stated conditions hold. Preserve necessary conditions and differences between mechanisms rather than merging them under a convenient label. If an essential condition is absent, establish a different defensible comparison with its own value or stop that transfer. Check material revisions against current prior work. An old paper's limitation may already have been resolved.

Record in English which pattern opened a question, changed its comparison or ruled out a transfer. Keep candidates originating in field or data opportunities labeled accordingly. If no pattern applies, explain that finding; do not attach an ornamental citation or erase the reviewed bank. A pattern offers a way to think, not proof of novelty or answerability.
