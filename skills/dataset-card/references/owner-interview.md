# Ask only for missing owner knowledge

First inspect authorized documentation and code. Bundle the few questions that block progress; continue independent work while awaiting replies. Do not require the owner to invent a research question.

Possible questions:

1. What did the collection aim to measure, and what is the independent unit?
2. Which conditions were deliberately separated, randomized or confounded by design?
3. Which variable meanings, clocks, units or sentinel values are easy to misread?
4. What published or ongoing analyses already use these data?
5. What inclusion and quality-control rules were applied?
6. Are there known changes in equipment, preprocessing or acquisition timing?
7. Which measurements were collected together on the same units?
8. Are additional measurements documented but stored elsewhere, and are they in scope?
9. Which possible uses are known to be unsupported by the design?
10. Which data are reserved, excluded or not authorized for inspection?

Record answers as owner reports with their scope. An owner report is useful evidence, but do not label it as an executed check. A missing response remains unknown; it does not block unrelated structural work.
