# Independently review a question-pattern bank

Read the collection objective and actual pattern guide first. Within the assigned budget, try its question-forming and, where present, hypothesis-forming moves in an explicitly described setting different from the supporting case. Record the starting scientific situation, the actual operation selected from the guide, the question or hypothesis it enabled, and the scientific steps you had to supply. Include an intended valid use as well as a failed transfer where meaningful; report whether the guide helps in the intended case and why a transfer succeeds or fails. Label a fictional setting and use it to assess clarity or logic; assess scientific support from the papers. Then read the supporting cases, source index and relevant primary sources. This short use check belongs to the existing review, not another discovery run. Use a fresh context separate from the author; for an initial review, do not receive the author's preferred verdict or prior reviewer conclusions. For reuse, the existing review is evidence about its specific revision, not proof that today's content is unchanged. Write English findings to the assigned bank `review.md` or a separate project applicability review.

Reconstruct the original scientific question and decisive comparison for patterns used or presented as established. Verify that the case accurately describes observations, methods, results and supported inference. Separately challenge the consequential inference: with the stated conditions satisfied, could a different process produce the decisive observation without the claimed conclusion? Use a source passage, short derivation or concrete counterexample where appropriate. Also distinguish this challenge from a failed transfer in which a required condition is missing. Keep the two outcomes distinct, and preserve a worthwhile question when only its proposed mechanism explanation fails. Distinguish the paper's tested alternatives from broader alternatives; inspect strong source claims as well as the bank's reconstruction. Distinguish an author limitation, a later resolved issue, and a current open problem. Check whether supposedly independent examples are genuinely different supporting cases rather than multiple descriptions of one study. Preserve abstract-only or inaccessible-source limits.

Ask whether the synthesis teaches an actionable way to form a scientifically worthwhile question and, where applicable, a hypothesis with distinguishing predictions. Check the trigger, transformation, comparison and scientific consequence; identify broad families that need clearer branches. Hypotheses can coexist, and different mechanisms can predict the same observation. Preserve useful descriptive or predictive aims without inventing rival mechanisms. Can you apply its reasoning to a different setting without inventing missing essentials? Try a concrete setting where the move would fail. If the guide omits the reason for failure, ask for that condition to be restored. Distinguish broad labels such as 'look at heterogeneity' from a specific comparison and the scientific ambiguity it resolves. Do not demand every pattern be universal or mechanically force a pattern onto every future question.

For a reused bank, compare relevant current claims with the revision actually reviewed and inspect supporting cases/sources. Review changed scientific meaning and the proposed project's correspondence; unchanged source-backed content can retain its earlier review with disclosed access limits. Record the precise scope of this check. If the old revision or key source cannot be inspected, do not claim fresh source verification; carry the affected uncertainty into reuse. An unchanged filename alone is insufficient.

Record paths/revisions, evidence read, consequential defects and minimal repairs, supported/provisional patterns and access limitations. Check whether the project selection preserves essential conditions without claiming feasibility in advance. Return findings to the coordinator without editing author files or spawning children. Recheck substantive repairs under the same [bounded critique](../../discover/references/critique.md) allowance, including a replacement rule's scientific validity and the conclusions that follow from it. Inspect newly added or strengthened claims in the repaired argument, even when the requested correction concerned only a count, condition or source detail. State the evidence for their supported scope and whether the remaining question-forming contribution still matters.
