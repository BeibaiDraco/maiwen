# Data and privacy

Maiwen consists of skills, Markdown guidance and an optional local script for inspecting data structure. It has no hosted service, user account, telemetry, MCP server, model gateway or automatic upload hook.

Your coding-agent host processes the requests and the context it reads. Local data, paper passages and research notes may become model input under the host's configuration and provider policies. Keeping files on your computer therefore does not mean inference stays on your computer. Connected search, browser and data tools may send requests to their own services.

Set the permitted data scope in the host and the project's brief before starting. The structural profiler reports structure by default; research planning may also require counts, distributions and coverage. Permission to read private material does not by itself permit redistribution.

Research outputs are files in your chosen workspace. They may contain sensitive scientific information even when they contain no raw data. Review them before sharing or committing them. Removing the plugin leaves the research workspace and host history in place; manage storage and provider processing through the relevant settings.

Access restrictions are enforced by the host, operating system and connected services. See [Support](SUPPORT.md) for reporting a problem involving sensitive material.
