# Data and privacy

Maiwen is a collection of skills, Markdown guidance and an optional local structural profiler. It does not operate a hosted service, require a Maiwen account, collect telemetry or bundle an MCP server, external model gateway or automatic upload hook.

Your coding-agent host processes the requests and the context it reads. Local data, paper passages and saved notes may become model context under that host's configuration and provider policies. Local files do not imply local-only inference. Connected search, browser or data tools may send requests to their respective services.

Choose the permitted dataset scope in the host and the project's brief. Review access to private or restricted research before starting. The structural profiler reports structure by default; planning may also need authorized counts, distributions and coverage. Reading a file and redistributing it are different permissions.

Research outputs are ordinary files in the workspace you choose. They can contain sensitive scientific information, even when raw data are absent. Review them before sharing or committing them. Plugin removal does not revoke provider-side processing or erase your workspace/history; manage those through your host and storage settings.

Maiwen's instructions help organize this work. Actual access restrictions are enforced by the host, operating system and connected services. See [Support](SUPPORT.md) for reporting an issue without publishing sensitive material.
